---
title: "Contributor Code of Conduct"
---

As contributors and maintainers of this project,
we pledge to follow the [The Carpentries Code of Conduct][coc].

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior
may be reported by following our [reporting guidelines][coc-reporting].

When this lesson is used in the context of Oregon State University
instructors and students will follow [Oregon State University 
policies and standards][osu-policies], and [Oregon State University
Code of Student Conduct][osu-student].

[coc-reporting]: https://docs.carpentries.org/policies/coc/incident-reporting.html
[coc]: https://docs.carpentries.org/policies/coc/
[osu-policies]: https://policy.oregonstate.edu/policy-standards-manual
[osu-student]: https://scs.oregonstate.edu/code-student-conduct
